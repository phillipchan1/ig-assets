Esther and I call it high authority, high affection. It's not a formula, just our attempt at raising God-fearing, high-EQ, well-behaved, very loved kids.

Authority makes kids feel safe. Affection makes them feel loved. They need both.

But the moment that matters most is when they're hurting. That's where trust is won or lost. We learned it from God himself. At Israel's lowest moment, he didn't lead with more authority. He said, "My presence will go with you" (Exodus 33:14).

Which comes more naturally to you, authority or affection?
