When God feels far, we usually assume it's on his end.

Sometimes it is. But a lot of the time, the distance is a wall made of things we've never said to him. The disappointment. The question we're embarrassed to still be asking. The frustration we've decided isn't appropriate to bring up.

In one of my hardest seasons, things didn't turn when I prayed better. They turned when I finally said the deep things out loud.

What haven't you told him?
