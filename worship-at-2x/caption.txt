I listen to almost everything at 2x. Podcasts, sermons, YouTube. So when a worship song came on in the car at double speed, part of my brain actually asked, "Does God's presence come twice as fast?"

It doesn't. God isn't content. He's a person, and every person has a pace.

Less tapping. Less scrolling. Less numbing. More stillness, silence, Sabbath, and Scripture.

What's one thing you could slow down this week?
