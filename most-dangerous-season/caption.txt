Failure is loud. It drives you to God.
Waiting is quiet. That's what makes it dangerous.

In Exodus 32, Israel didn't build an idol because they hated God. They built it because Moses was "delayed." Forty days of silence was more than they could stand. And they still called it a feast to the LORD.

Most of us will never bow down to a statue. But a lot of us, me included, know what it's like to take the good things God gave us and lean on them when he feels slow.

What are you building while you wait?
